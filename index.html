Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Your data stays on your device

DayZero does not collect, transmit or store any personal data on external servers. Everything you enter — your name, habit, reason, cravings — is saved exclusively on your device.

What we do not collect

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, usage data, or any information that could identify you. DayZero works entirely offline and does not require an internet connection to function.

Backups

If you use the backup feature, your data is exported as a JSON file to a folder you choose on your device (for example Google Drive on Android). This file is created and managed entirely by you — we never access it.

Payments

DayZero is free for everyone, with no in-app purchases or subscriptions. If you choose to leave an optional tip on Ko-fi, the payment is handled entirely by Ko-fi and PayPal — no financial information is ever shared with DayZero.

Notifications

DayZero may send local notifications to support your streak. These are scheduled entirely on your device and do not involve any external server.

Children

DayZero is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

For any questions about privacy, write to: dayzero.sup@hotmail.com
